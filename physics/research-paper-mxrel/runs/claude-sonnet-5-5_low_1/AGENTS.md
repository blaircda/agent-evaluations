# Goal
Read and comment on a scientific paper (string theory).

# The paper
The paper is paper.tex

# Instructions
Read the paper and in the working directory draw up a short .tex file called report.tex, and compile this to report.pdf using pdflatex. In this report, give:
1) A brief assessment of the principal achievement of the paper
2) A brief assessment of the principal drawback of the paper
3) Balancing feasibility and impact, what research idea building on the paper is most worth pursuing?

Do not create or modify files outside the working directory.
